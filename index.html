About

I am a Master's student in Computer Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Hai-Tao Zheng. My research lies in Natural Language Processing, with a particular focus on Large Language Models. I am currently a Senior Researcher (Top Talent) at the LongCat Team of Meituan, where I study how agents can reliably complete long-horizon tasks in complex systems. I am also a contributor to Ling-3.0-flash and Ling-3.1-flash at Ant Group, whose official model cards have received 500+ likes and 15,000+ monthly downloads on Hugging Face.
